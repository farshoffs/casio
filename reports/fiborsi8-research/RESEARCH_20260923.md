## Fixed execution convention

- XAUUSD Dukascopy BID M5.
- RSI period 8.
- Signal timeframe depends on experiment.
- Higher-timeframe RSI uses completed bars only.
- Entry occurs only after the signal candle closes.
- Stop = opposite wick of the signal candle.
- Target = fixed 3R unless stated otherwise.
- One position at a time.
- If SL and TP are touched in the same M5 bar, SL is counted first.
- Starting balance RM100.
- Risk = 5% of current equity per trade.
- No BE or protected-SL manipulation.
- Spread/commission/slippage not modeled.

## Research decision

REJECT the 23/77 2026 threshold sweet spot as a standalone production strategy.

The public RSI8 + trigger candle + one-HTF filter implementation has a clear 2026 regime edge but does not survive 2024/2025.

The unresolved component is the proprietary/undocumented Fibonacci structure (PR/R1/R2/TP2) or another causal market-regime filter. Future work should focus on reconstructing a causal structural gate rather than optimizing RSI thresholds further.
