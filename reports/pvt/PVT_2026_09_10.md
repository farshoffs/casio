## Rules / backtest assumptions

A self-contained JS port of the PVT-inspired TradingView strategy
\`PVT_XAUUSD_Sweep_Confirmation_v1.pine\`, tested on 5-minute candles:

1. Track Asian high and low, 00:00–07:00 UTC.
2. Only seek entries 07:00–11:00 and 12:30–16:00 UTC.
3. Sweep one range extreme, close back inside, wick >= 25%, overshoot >= 0.05 ATR.
4. Store rejected sweep for up to 3 subsequent bars. Confirmation must break sweep candle opposite extreme, be directional and have body >= 45%.
5. With volume enabled: prior 6-bar average relative volume <= 1.0, sweep RVOL <= 1.5, confirmation RVOL >= 1.1.
6. With H1 filter enabled: most recently **completed** H1 close, EMA50, EMA200 align with trade.
7. Stop outside sweep by 0.15 ATR or min 0.35 ATR; max stop 3.0 ATR; TP 2.3 times price risk.
8. Risk 0.25% of simulated equity per trade, initial $2,500, quantity rounded down in 0.01-ounce steps, max 2 signals/day.
9. Signals formed at M5 close; orders enter on the **next M5 open**; stop wins if stop and target both touch one M5 bar.
10. **Assumed synthetic research friction of $0.50/oz round trip**, covering spread and slippage in aggregate: half allocated against entry, half against exit. True FXPro spread, commission, swaps, liquidity, and TradingView's intrabar emulator are not reproduced.

Results are rounded USD values for this position-size model, **not broker-lot or funded-account payout claims**.

## Conclusions / next scientific tests

1. The original rule combination is too restrictive in these periods. It cannot be judged on wins or PF when **no trades occur**.
2. Removing **only** volume permits two trades, but has insufficient sample size.
3. Removing both volume and H1 trend increases signal count, yet has slightly negative expectancy with the assumed costs. It should not be promoted to live trading.
4. Test adjacent months **with the same settings** before parameter optimization. Later, evaluate controlled RVOL relaxation and alternate structural confirmation with walk-forward validation, not September-only curve-fitting.
5. For feed fidelity, repeat using the user's FXPro M1 CSV resampled to M5 once that full raw file is accessible. Tick volume from FXPro and Dukascopy's reported volume are not interchangeable.
6. Synchronize repository market data before claiming October beyond the last stored bar.
