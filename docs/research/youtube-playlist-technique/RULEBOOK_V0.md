# Paul-X Bermula / SNR Research Rulebook v0

Status: **evidence-derived draft — not yet backtest-ready**

This document consolidates recurring rules found across the supplied 14-video playlist. It deliberately separates creator teaching from CASIO operational hypotheses.

## 1. Technique identity

The source material is a support/resistance-based price-action method centered on Paul-X's **Bermula** concept: identify where a meaningful rise or fall began, use those areas as support/resistance or buyer/seller zones, observe breakout/reaction behavior, and use a lower timeframe for confirmation and execution.

External indexing of related Paul-X material also associates the family with SNR/FTR and role-reversal concepts such as SBR/RBS. Those external labels are contextual only; rules below are based on the supplied videos.

## 3. Preliminary MTF architecture

The strongest cross-video interpretation is:

```text
Higher TF
    identify meaningful S/R / Bermula origin
    determine directional context
          |
          v
Setup TF
    observe reaction OR breakout/displacement
    if breakout: require pullback/retest
    observe role reversal where applicable
          |
          v
Lower TF
    wait for confirmation
    execute near the relevant S/R / Bermula area
          |
          v
Risk
    SL beyond structural invalidation / zone
    target based on next structural objective in creator material
```

Tag: DERIVED. The exact fixed mapping (for example H4 -> H1 -> M5) is not yet proven as one universal creator requirement.

## 4. Breakout setup — creator-faithful skeleton

### Bearish example

1. Identify a meaningful support/origin area.
2. Require a strong downside breakout.
3. Wait for price to pull back toward the broken area.
4. Treat the broken support as potential resistance.
5. On a lower timeframe, wait for confirmation at/near the area.
6. Enter short only after the confirmation condition is satisfied.
7. Place a real stop beyond structural invalidation.

### Bullish example

1. Identify a meaningful resistance/origin area.
2. Require a strong upside breakout.
3. Wait for price to pull back toward the broken area.
4. Treat the broken resistance as potential support.
5. On a lower timeframe, wait for confirmation.
6. Enter long only after confirmation.
7. Place a real stop beyond structural invalidation.

Tags:
- Steps 1–6: DIRECT/DERIVED combination.
- Exact SL buffer: unresolved.

## 6. Conflicts and unresolved rules

### C001 — Does breakout require candle close?

Video 05 indicates that a break which closes back inside is not a valid breakout.

Video 13, however, includes a statement around ~00:04:34 that he may already call a move a breakout before the candle has closed.

Do not force a single rule yet. Possible explanations to test:
- “breakout identification” can be intrabar, but entry waits for later confirmation;
- strong displacement can be recognized before close, while close-back-inside invalidates it;
- context differs by timeframe/setup.

Status: unresolved.
